================================================================================
TROUBLESHOOTING
================================================================================

"MT5 Connection Failed"
  → Open MetaTrader 5 and login
  → Make sure terminal stays open while daemon runs

"No trades firing"
  → Check dashboard for velocity z-score
  → If z-score < 2.0, market velocity is weak
  → Check session (should be London or NY hours)

"Dashboard not loading"
  → Make sure daemon is fully started (look for "▶️  STARTING")
  → Try browser refresh
  → Check firewall isn't blocking port 8000

"Too many rejections"
  → Increase z-score threshold in default_config.py
  → Market may just be weak at that moment

"Trades staying open too long"
  → Lower health_health_threshold_exit in config
  → Or lower reversal_risk_threshold
